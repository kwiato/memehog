AI indexing (search inside memes)

Each night new memes are sent to a vision model that reads the text on them and writes a short description — both become searchable. Works with any OpenAI-compatible API; the free Google Gemini tier easily covers a personal library. Pick a preset, paste your key, hit Test, save.
